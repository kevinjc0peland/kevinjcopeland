01 / The story

Experience shared.
Possibility unlocked.

Entrepreneur. Educator. Family first.

For more than a decade with Influance Hair Care, I traveled, taught, and led seminars in the hair care industry. That experience shaped how I build: connect with people, share what works, and make the next step clear.

In 2019, I founded Kevin Copeland LLC (KCLLC) to bring that same approach to business and credit education. Today, I help everyday people understand how credit really works, take steps to improve it, and build businesses with a stronger foundation.

As a content creator and proud family man known as “The Special Needs CEO,” I document the journey along the way. The mission is simple: put proper information in people’s hands, create opportunity, and keep family first.
