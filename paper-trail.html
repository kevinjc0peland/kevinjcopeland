Mail room

1Print the letter from the Letter tab and sign it above your name.
2Print or copy your screenshots and anything else you are enclosing. The Print letter + screenshots button lays the whole packet out in one go. Keep a full copy of everything for your own file.
3At the post office, ask for Certified Mail with Return Receipt, so you get the green card or an electronic signature back as proof it landed and who signed for it.
4The clerk fills the certified slip with the recipient address, sticks it on the envelope, and hands you the receipt. Keep the receipt, it carries the tracking number.
5When the green card comes back, staple it to your copy of the letter. That pairing is your proof of service.
6Put the tracking number on the case here: edit the newest record for that party and drop it in the summary.
