What today taught me

Kevin Copeland

November 19 2026

I record a lot of these in the car. Sitting there today, thinking, and the thought hit me before I could even get the phone out. So let me give you what today taught me, while it's still warm.

The way today moves. Time is flying. People going here, people going there. Somebody asking you to do this. Somebody wanting to talk about that. You've got something you need to express over here. And the day just goes. It doesn't ask permission. It just goes.

And here's what I had to sit with. If you don't manage your time, somebody else will mismanage you for you.

Read that one again. Because that's not a saying, that's a schedule. Every hour you don't claim, somebody else claims it. Their emergency. Their question. Their errand. Their idea of what your time is for. They're not even doing it on purpose. But if you leave your day unlocked, the world will walk right in and rearrange your furniture.

So what do you do with that. If you've got goals, aspirations, dreams, and I know you do, map it out. Write it down. Add a plan. And then set timers. This is the part people skip. Set a timer. Take ten minutes and knock out that one task. Take five minutes and knock out that one task. Half that phone call. Document everything. One by one. Shot by shot. Everything adds up.

That's the whole secret people keep looking for. Not a hack. Not an app. Timers and small chunks and one by one. You'd be amazed what ten minutes does when it has a job. The small pieces feel like nothing, and then one day you look up and the thing is built. Everything adds up. One by one, shot by shot.

And the part that got me, sitting in that car. The lesson been in the car the whole time. I've been riding around with this one for years. Rushing, getting pulled, wondering where the day went. The answer was riding shotgun the entire time. Take care of your time like it's yours, because it is. Manage it. Map it. Chunk it. Set the timer and go. Because if I don't take care of it, nobody else is going to take care of it for me. And that's true for you too. Nobody's coming. That's not harsh, that's freeing. The day is yours if you'll hold it.

So that's what today taught me. Tomorrow I'll have another one for you.

I love y'all. Continue.
