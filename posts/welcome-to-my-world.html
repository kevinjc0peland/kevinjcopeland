Welcome to my world.

Day 1 of 30

Kevin Copeland

October 27 2026

Really, I don't have all of this figured out. I'm starting a new page. Rebranding. Trying to figure out where I want this direction to go. That's the honest place I'm starting from. Not a finished picture. Just me, opening up a little more than I usually do.

What I do know is I have a lot on my mind. Information. Research. Things I've learned. Experiences I've lived through. I'm trying to find a way to get those things out, because I know something in there can help somebody with what they're going through too.

But I don't open up a lot. I grew up taking speech. Sometimes I couldn't articulate my words the way I wanted to. I knew what I wanted to say, but getting it out was something I had to work on. It's something I'm still conscious of. Something I'm still growing in.

And I know that without me getting things out, it's going to be difficult for people to understand where I'm coming from. My positioning. My way of thinking. The ideas and the vision. How my brain actually works. Keeping all of that in my head doesn't give anybody much of a chance to understand me.

Recently, we've been learning a lot about how my son's brain works. He has autism, ADHD and a developmental delay. There are areas where he's a year or two behind where he's expected to be. Not having a steady teacher has made that process harder. We're in the process of starting ABA therapy, and speech has been going great. There is a lot we're learning as we go.

Some of that learning has made me think about myself too. I believe I have ADHD as well. And this year, I found out my brother took speech. Then I found out my mom took speech. Those were things I didn't know about my own family.

You'll be surprised what you don't know until certain questions come up. A conversation can show you something that was there all along. Something you hadn't understood about the people around you, or about yourself.

That's one thing I appreciate about speaking on yourself. When you tell other people about your own experience, you're giving them the invitation to be open about themselves. The conversation has somewhere to go. It doesn't have to be perfect to mean something.

It's a great thing to learn. It's a great thing to continue to grow. And it's a great thing to continue to be yourself. There's nothing wrong with the way you're made. There's nothing wrong with the way you operate. Growing doesn't mean I have to stop being me.

So, welcome to my world. This is where I'm starting. A little more open. Still learning how to get the words out. Still figuring out the direction, but letting people understand more of the person behind it.

More of this journey will unfold as the series continues.
