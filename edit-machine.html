    Engine details
    

    


    V1 notes: Word-level transcription runs locally (a quantized Whisper tiny English model). Pauses longer than the cut threshold shrink to the keep amount; cuts never touch a word; timestamps remap to the trimmed timeline. Captions burn as 2–4 word beats, big bold white with a navy outline, Carolina blue on your pop words, in the lower third; the header line sits at the top for 2.5 seconds. The video ends on the last spoken word with a hard cut, no outro card. Export is 720 × 1280 or 1080 × 1920, 30 fps, H.264 with AAC. The speech model runs on your device: a quiet computer takes roughly a minute per minute of tape, and you should check the transcript before cutting. Music or heavy background noise throws off word timing. Nothing is saved between visits. Use current desktop Chrome or Edge; a long tape at 1080 can exceed browser memory. MOV codecs vary; an unsupported source shows an error. Files over 500 MB are blocked.
