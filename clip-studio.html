Engine check and details

Single-threaded ffmpeg.wasm. No SharedArrayBuffer or cross-origin isolation required. The check makes a tiny synthetic video and runs a captioned vertical export locally.



V1 notes: Center crop, 720 × 1280, H.264 MP4 with AAC audio when present. Captions are bold white Arial with a navy outline/shadow in the lower third (system Arial fallback where unavailable). No automatic transcription, tracking, accounts, API keys or subscriptions. Use current desktop Chrome or Edge for best results; Safari, Firefox and mobile may have memory or codec limits. MOV codecs vary; an unsupported source will show an error. Files over 500 MB are blocked in v1; even smaller files can exceed browser memory. Downloads remain in this tab until refresh; save them before leaving. Nothing is saved between visits. Long captions shrink to fit; preview the output before posting. A ZIP temporarily uses extra memory.
